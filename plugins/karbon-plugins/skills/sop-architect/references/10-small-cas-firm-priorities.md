# Small CAS firm priorities (1-10 staff, bookkeeping/CAS focus)

This is the priority lens for the user's firm. Always load this in Phase 4 (prioritization).

The user's firm is **small (1-10 staff), bookkeeping and CAS focused**. The framework's 16 domains apply, but the gap-filling order is specific to this profile. Work down this list when ranking gaps.

---

## Tier 1 — Compliance and security (non-negotiable)

These are legal/regulatory exposures. Even at maturity Level 1, these must be in place. **Do these first regardless of effort.**

| # | SOP | Authority | Why first |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **WISP / Information Security Program** | IRS Pub 4557, FTC Safeguards §314 | Mandatory for PTIN renewal effective 1/1/2023; up to ~$46,517/violation/day FTC penalty; criminal §7216 referral |
| 2 | **Client confidentiality / IRC §7216 consent procedures** | IRC §7216, §6713, Treas. Reg. §301.7216-3 | Criminal misdemeanor exposure; required before ANY offshore work or third-party sharing |
| 3 | **Engagement letter SOP (with annual reissuance)** | AICPA Code, CAMICO | >50% of malpractice claims tie to missing/unclear engagement letters |
| 4 | **AI usage policy** | NIST AI RMF, AICPA §1.700, IRC §7216, Texas HB 149 | Public LLM use with client data is the most common active compliance violation in 2026 |
| 5 | **Breach response / incident response plan** | FTC §314.4(h), NIST 800-61 | Required if NPPI on >5,000 consumers; best practice regardless |

**Notes:**

- WISP and AI policy are often the two most-overlooked. The user almost certainly needs both.
- The small-entity exemption under FTC §314.6 applies if NPPI on <5,000 consumers — confirm with the user. The exemption removes only 4 of the 9 elements; the rest (Qualified Individual, encryption, MFA, training, breach notification, etc.) still apply.

## How to use this list in Phase 4

1. **Take the gap list from Phase 3.**
2. **Map each gap to its tier** (1-7) using this document.
3. **Order within each tier** by composite score (Risk + Frequency + Impact + Effort).
4. **Compliance gaps in Tier 1 always come first** regardless of composite score.
5. **Group output:**
   - **Immediate (next 30 days)** — Tier 1 items + any Tier 2-3 items with composite ≥10
   - **Near-term (next 90 days)** — Remaining Tier 2-3, top of Tier 4
   - **Backlog** — Tier 5-7 unless triggered by specific firm circumstances

## Defaults to assume for this firm (small, bookkeeping/CAS)

Unless the user states otherwise:

- **Probably has PTINs** (any tax work) → Pub 4557 / FTC §314 applies
- **Probably <5,000 NPPI consumers** → small-entity exemption likely applies (4 of 9 FTC elements relaxed)
- **No attest work** unless user confirms — independence rules attenuate but SQMS 1 still applies firm-wide
- **Owner-bottlenecked** ("octopus partner") — common at this size; many SOPs will surface this
- **Tribal knowledge** — most processes are known but not written; Phase 1 inventory will be light
- **Tech stack** — likely QBO/Xero + a workflow tool (Jetpack/Financial Cents) + Bill or Ramp + Liscio or Karbon + Fathom/LiveFlow + SmartVault
- **Some AI usage** — almost certainly using AI somewhere, even informally; AI policy gap is near-universal at this size
- **Succession planning likely absent** — only 7% of solo firms have it; surface as a Tier 6 priority
