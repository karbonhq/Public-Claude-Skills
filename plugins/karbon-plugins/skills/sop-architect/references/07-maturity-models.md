# Maturity models — frameworks for finding gaps

Load this in Phase 3 (gap analysis) and Phase 4 (prioritization). Use these models to assess where the firm is now and what "good" looks like at the next maturity step.

---

## CMMI-style five levels (applied to accounting firms)

| Level | Name | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Initial / Ad Hoc** | Work is reactive, individual-driven, undocumented. Outcomes depend on heroes. |
| 2 | **Repeatable** | Recurring work has been recognized; informal patterns emerge but aren't written down. |
| 3 | **Defined** | Standard processes documented as SOPs. Most work follows them. |
| 4 | **Managed (Quantitatively)** | Metrics drive process management. Realization, cycle time, defect rates measured. |
| 5 | **Optimizing** | Continuous improvement. Lean Six Sigma. Process owners drive iteration. |

**Most small firms (1-10 staff) sit at Level 1-2.** This skill aims to move the firm from Level 1-2 to Level 3 by producing the SOP backbone.

---

## CPA.com CAS Maturity Model

Runs from foundational transactional work to advisory:

1. **Transactional** — data entry, recs, report generation
2. **Controller-level** — month-end close, financial reporting, oversight of bookkeeping team
3. **Advisory** — KPI analysis, scenario planning, vCFO services, year-round dialogue with client

The CAS Service Maturity Model has two big buckets:

- **Financial CAS** — cloud-based monthly accounting and controllership oversight
- **Business Insights CAS** — non-financial data, industry-specific KPIs, year-round advisory dialogue

## CAS 2.0® framework (the gap-finding lens)

Four pillars — for each pillar, ask "what SOPs are missing?"

| Pillar | What's in scope | Common SOP gaps |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategy & Governance** | CAS business plan, niche definition, tier strategy, partner alignment | No written CAS plan; vague niche; no tier definitions |
| **Practice Development** | Marketing, lead-gen, sales process, ICP, proposal flow | No ICP rubric; no proposal SOP; ad-hoc lead handling |
| **Technology Solutions** | Tech stack, integrations, system-of-record decisions | App fatigue; no SoR per data type; tools not aligned to methodology |
| **Operational Excellence** | Methodology blueprinting, **process and procedure documentation**, quality control, training | No SOP library; tribal knowledge; no QC checklists |

The fourth pillar explicitly identifies **"methodology blueprinting" and "process and procedure documentation"** as Operational Excellence requirements. This skill is squarely a CAS 2.0 Pillar 4 tool.

## CAS Firm Self-Assessment Tool

A structured instrument from CPA.com and Succession Institute. Requires **two CAS stakeholders** to assess:

- Pricing
- Growth paths
- Leadership / governance / strategy
- Client management
- Accountability
- Current and long-term offerings

Useful as a pre-Phase-3 checklist with the firm's principals.

## AICPA Trusted Business Advisor® diagnostic

Surfaces services not currently offered to clients who need them. Useful for finding **service gaps** (which then create SOP gaps) — the firm should be doing more for clients than it currently is.

## Maturity progression in gap-finding (by firm size)

### Small firms (1-10) — the user's profile

**Where gaps usually live:**

- Client onboarding
- Scope and pricing
- AI policy

**Recommended diagnostics:**

- CPA.com CAS Self-Assessment
- Boomer Process Circle Readiness Assessment

**Prioritize 5-10 critical SOPs:**

1. Month-end close
2. Accounts receivable / collections
3. Client onboarding
4. Engagement letter
5. WISP / data security (Pub 4557 mandatory)
6. AI usage policy
7. Tax workflow (if PTIN)
8. Pricing tiers
9. Document collection
10. Disengagement

**E-Myth lens:** shift founder from technician to entrepreneur (Gerber & Root, *The E-Myth Accountant*).

### Mid-sized firms (10-50)

**Where gaps usually live:**

- Handoffs between roles
- Capacity management
- Consistency across teams

**Approach:**

- Value Stream Mapping (VSM) each major service line
- Appoint a Process Owner
- Boomer Process Circle candidates
- CAS 2.0 framework guides advisory buildout

### Large firms (50+)

**Where gaps usually live:**

- Integration points (cross-office, cross-service-line, M&A)

**Approach:**

- NIST AI RMF + COSO ICIF for GenAI become enterprise requirements
- ISO 9001 / ISO 42001 alignment
- Internal audit of process compliance
- CMMI Level 4-5 quantitative management
- Dedicated process improvement function

## Prioritization scoring (used in Phase 4)

For each gap, score on four dimensions:

| Dimension | High (3) | Medium (2) | Low (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | Compliance/legal exposure (FTC, IRC §7216, malpractice) | Material financial loss | Inconvenience |
| **Frequency** | Daily/weekly | Monthly/quarterly | Annual or one-off |
| **Impact if undocumented** | Client harm, key-person collapse, audit failure | Recurring rework, occasional errors | Internal friction only |
| **Effort to write** | Inverse: High (1), Medium (2), Low (3) — easier = higher priority | | |

**Composite score** = Risk + Frequency + Impact + Effort. Range 4-12.

**Prioritization buckets:**

- **Immediate (10-12)** — do in next 30 days
- **Near-term (7-9)** — next 90 days
- **Backlog (4-6)** — later

Risk of compliance exposure (Pub 4557, FTC §314, IRC §7216, SQMS 1) **always** wins, regardless of composite score — these are non-negotiable.

## When to mark a domain "N/A"

A domain is N/A only if the firm legitimately doesn't do that work:

| Domain | Mark N/A only if... |
|---|---|
| 1 Lifecycle | Never. Every firm has a lifecycle. |
| 2 Doc collection | Never. Every firm collects client data. |
| 3 Pricing | Never. |
| 4 Transaction processing | Firm doesn't do bookkeeping for any client |
| 5 Reconciliations | Same as 4 |
| 6 Close | Same as 4 |
| 7 Reporting | Firm only does tax (no monthly reporting) |
| 8 Payroll/sales tax | Firm doesn't touch payroll or sales tax — rare |
| 9 Advisory/CAS | Firm doesn't offer CAS — common for tax-only practices |
| 10 QC / review | Never. SQMS 1 applies firm-wide. |
| 11 Workflow / practice mgmt | Never. |
| 12 Tech stack | Never. |
| 13 People / RACI | Solo practitioner with no contractors |
| 14 Internal ops | Never. |
| 15 Risk / compliance / security | Never. WISP mandatory if any PTIN. |
| 16 AI | Never. Even "we don't use AI" must be documented. |

For a small bookkeeping/CAS firm, expect **0-2 domains N/A.** If the firm doesn't do any tax prep, some of Pub 4557/§7216 may attenuate (though FTC §314 may still apply).
