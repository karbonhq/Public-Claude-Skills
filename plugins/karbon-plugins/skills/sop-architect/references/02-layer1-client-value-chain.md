# Layer 1 — Client value chain (Domains 1-3)

The client-facing operating contract. Load this when mapping or building SOPs for client lifecycle, document collection, or pricing/billing.

---

## Domain 1 — Client lifecycle

**Core principle:** select the customer first, then design the service. Ron Baker: "you don't price the service; you price the customer." The lifecycle is the operating contract — every later SOP depends on the engagement letter being correct.

### Seven lifecycle stages (each warrants its own SOP or sub-section)

1. **Prospecting and lead qualification** — ICP scoring rubric, A/B/C grading, automated routing
2. **Scoping and discovery** — pre-call questionnaire, software inventory, risk flags. SmartPath's Ideal Client Discovery Blueprint© is a public reference.
3. **Proposal and engagement letters** — see below
4. **Onboarding (30/60/90 plan)** — see below
5. **Ongoing service delivery rhythms** — monthly close meeting, QBR, etc.
6. **Change orders** — see below
7. **Disengagement** — explicit termination letter (failure to disengage formally creates ongoing liability)

### Engagement letters (highest-leverage SOP in the firm)

CNA / AICPA Professional Liability data: **>50% of malpractice claims involve a missing engagement letter; 75-77% of 2023-2024 claims involved tax services.**

**Mandatory clauses** (synthesized from AICPA, MICPA, CAMICO):

- Purpose of the engagement
- Scope and **explicit exclusions** (FinCEN/BOI was the 2024 example)
- Client responsibilities and deadlines
- Reliance on client-provided facts
- Fee terms and late fees
- Stop-work clause
- Record retention
- Third-party service provider language (incl. offshore disclosure under IRC §7216 if applicable)
- Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) clause
- Limitation of liability
- Termination terms (typically 30-day notice)

The standard AICPA individual letter is **8 pages plus an 8-page T&C addendum.**

**Anti-patterns to forbid:**

- Evergreen letters (must be reissued annually)
- Superlatives ("all," "every," "verify")
- Marketing language inside the letter

**SSARS 27 affirmative election** (effective Dec 15, 2026): every CAS engagement letter should now affirmatively elect or disclaim under SSARS, document the SKE designation per ET §1.295, and follow the *1136 Tenants* CAMICO-cited rule of explicit scope.

### Onboarding (second-highest-leverage SOP)

Industry average ~60 days; top firms <30 days. Most churn happens in first 90 days (Karbon).

**Standard 12-element onboarding checklist:**

1. Signed engagement letter and first invoice issued (autopay required)
2. Welcome email with named account manager
3. Accountant-level access (not client login) to QBO/Xero
4. Bank/CC feeds connected
5. Payroll/AP/AR/sales-tax mapped
6. Chart of accounts cleanup
7. Document collection of prior returns and beneficial-owner data
8. AR/AP aging review
9. Practice-management setup
10. Structured 60-minute kickoff
11. Initial financial-health assessment as the first proactive deliverable
12. Documented 30/60/90 plan

### Scope creep (measurable revenue leak)

Ignition's State of Client Engagement: 89% of accountants delay difficult fee conversations; 43% absorb the cost. Scopekeeper estimates ~20% annual revenue loss.

**Highest-ROI intervention:** Intuit Firm of the Future "Extra Work Order" pad — one firm surfaced **$72,000/year** of previously free work.

**Standard SOP rules:**

- Written change-order template (cost, time, description)
- 70%-of-budget alert triggers partner review
- 10-15% built-in contingency in fixed-fee pricing
- Engagement letter clause requiring written amendment

### Common gaps in Domain 1

- No pre-call qualification (discovery becomes unpaid consulting)
- Evergreen letters
- Verbal "yes" to scope expansion without paper trail
- "Octopus partner" pattern (every request routes through owner)
- Onboarding past 60 days
- No defined ICP
- No formal disengagement letter (ongoing liability)
- No QBR cadence (clients perceive "I just get a P&L by email")

## Domain 2 — Document & data collection

**Core principle:** one source of truth, one secure portal, never email attachments. Required by **IRS Pub 4557, FTC Safeguards Rule, AICPA hosting interpretation §1.295.143.**

### SOP components

- **Portal selection** — Liscio, SmartVault (SOC 2 Type II), ShareFile, Karbon Client Tasks, Canopy Client Portal, TaxDome, Hubdoc, Dext, Content Snare
- **PBC list** refreshed monthly with **95% completeness gate** before close starts
- **3-step follow-up cadence** (Day 5/10/15) followed by manager-to-client escalation
- **Quarterly access recertification**
- **Retention schedule** — see below

### Retention defaults

| Standard | Retention |
|---|---|
| AICPA recommendation | 7 years |
| IRS general | 3 years |
| IRS >25% omission | 6 years |
| IRS worthless securities / bad debt | 7 years |
| IRS fraud | Indefinite |
| SAS 103 (audit) | 5 years from report release |
| Permanent (per ICPAS) | Depreciation schedules, COA, by-laws, trial balances |

### Common gaps in Domain 2

- Documents collected via email (direct violation of Pub 4557 and FTC Safeguards)
- No written WISP
- Indefinite "keep everything" retention (creates blast-radius liability)
- Failure to return original client records under Circular 230 §10.28
- Missed annual W-9 refresh leading to 24% backup withholding under IRC §3406

## Domain 3 — Pricing & billing

**Core principle:** sell intellectual capital, not time. Ron Baker's *Implementing Value Pricing* and *The Firm of the Future* are the canonical texts. Hourly billing "penalizes innovation, ingenuity and creativity and rewards inefficiency, hoarding work and non-effective delegation."

The 2024 CAS Benchmark Survey: firms that "right-price work with fixed-fee strategies billed on a recurring basis" outpace peers.

### Three-tier subscription pricing (dominant pattern)

| Tier | Service | Typical monthly fee |
|---|---|---|
| Essential / Bronze | Bookkeeping only | $400-$1,500 |
| Strategic / Silver | + Controllership | $1,500-$5,000 |
| Comprehensive / Gold | + vCFO with 13-week cash forecasting | $5,000-$15,000+ |

**The "Magic of Three"** anchors most clients to the middle (66-77% select middle tier per Botkeeper-cited research). Rule: gap between Gold and Silver should exceed gap between Silver and Bronze.

**Cleanup is always priced separately** and completed before recurring work starts.

### VeraSage Value Pricing Formula (Baker)

Hours × rate as a cost floor, then add explicit premiums:

| Premium | Add |
|---|---|
| Fixed price | +12.6% |
| Change-order discipline | +3.1% |
| Service guarantee | +22.2% |
| Price guarantee | +16.3% |
| Cyclical-cash-flow payment terms | +14.7% |

### Operational benchmarks (billing hygiene)

| Metric | Target | Concerning |
|---|---|---|
| Realization | ≥85% (top >90%) | <85% |
| Write-off rate | <5% | 5-10% concerning, >10% indicates pricing/staffing/client-selection problem |
| WIP days | <30 best-in-class | >45 |
| AR days | <30 excellent | >60 |
| Lockup days (WIP + AR) | <45 best-in-class | >90 = cash-flow crisis |
| Collection rate | ≥98% | <95% |
| Firm profit margin | 18.4% avg, 25-30% top | <15% |

CAS NCFPP median **$156,250** (2024 benchmark, +29% over 2022).

### Common gaps in Domain 3

- Per-hour pricing with no defined deliverables
- Free payroll absorbed inside bookkeeping packages
- One-tier menus with no anchor
- Carrying inflated WIP/AR for fear of bad realization optics
- No annual price review

## Cross-domain dependencies

- Domain 1 (engagement letter) depends on Domain 3 (pricing tier and fee terms)
- Domain 2 (data collection) depends on Domain 1 (engagement letter explicit data scope)
- Domain 1 (onboarding) depends on Domains 2, 4, 5, 6 (the work the engagement enables must have its own SOPs)
