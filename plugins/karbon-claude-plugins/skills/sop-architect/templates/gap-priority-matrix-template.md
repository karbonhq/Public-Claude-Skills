## Scoring rubric

| Dimension | High (3) | Medium (2) | Low (1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | Compliance/legal exposure (FTC, IRC §7216, malpractice, SQMS 1) | Material financial loss | Inconvenience |
| **Frequency** | Daily/weekly | Monthly/quarterly | Annual or one-off |
| **Impact if undocumented** | Client harm, key-person collapse, audit failure | Recurring rework, occasional errors | Internal friction only |
| **Effort to write** (inverse) | Low complexity (3) | Medium (2) | High (1) |

**Composite** = Risk + Frequency + Impact + Effort. Range 4-12.

**Compliance gaps in Tier 1 always come first** regardless of composite score.
